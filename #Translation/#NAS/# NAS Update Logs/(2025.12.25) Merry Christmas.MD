Merry Christmas! As a little gift, I added in magic diapers with the ability to attune regular ones with your Youjutsu powers. These activate when peed or pooped in, and can flip battles towards your advantage. This should hopefully spice-up endgame battles where both combatants have Excretion Magic. Because being the recipient to Coprodyne spam isn't fun.

As starters, you will need Basic Summoning or Excretion Youjutsu. Either is required, you don't need both. Once you have said Youjutsu learned, you can go into the underwear change menu which you can enter by using either the Relieve Self command or the Diaper Play command during sex.

There will now be a preferred magic type option which will allow you to change what attunement you want to put on the underwear. Clicking on "Preferred magic type" will enter the magic attunement screen.

The magic attunement screen will list how the magic underwear attunement system works, the two selections, Self for your underwear that you change yourself, and Others for any underwear that you change, usually for diaper change requests where you can put a little booby trap in the diaper of the victim.

Speaking of attunements, there are 11 of them, all of the main damaging magic types such as fire, ice, hypnosis, etc, a few couple of buffs such as Dia and Tarukaja, and several negative attunements that you can do:
- Neyou: Knocks the wearer out on an accident, akin to fainting. Yes, if you're stupid enough, you can attune it to your own diaper which will result in you fainting if you soil it.
- Lock: The classic cursed clothing attunement. This makes the underwear impossible to remove normally unless by the curser. To uncurse, you will need to have 「Agero!」 which can be unlocked on B rank of the Basic Summoning Youjutsu.
- Murimuri: Aphrodisiac, gradually increases arousal when wearing.

Once your diapers have been tuned to the magic that you want to use, it's time to get yourself bursting. Drink all of the elixirs you have and bulk up on laxatives and wait until you're basically at your limit. Once in combat you can either keep holding it in until you inevitably leak due to damage or going over your limit, or by mercifully using the Soil Self/Use Diaper command.

The typical accident event will play out, with a spell casting afterwards. The spell casted will depend on the type of accident and amount leaked proportionate to your max bladder/bowel capacity. Pee accidents cast single-target spells, while poopy accidents cast multi-target ones. The stronger the accident, the stronger spell it will cast. Dual accidents cast twice.

Soiled magic diapers can still cast spells, but when they hit their limit and are leaking or blown out, they will no longer cast spells and you will need to change into a new one.

TIP: If you use an excretion spell on yourself such as Oureindyne, your accident will be much stronger.
