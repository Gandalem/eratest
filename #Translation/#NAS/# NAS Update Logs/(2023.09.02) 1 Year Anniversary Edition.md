# eraNAS HAS BEEN IN DEVELOPMENT FOR A YEAR!
For our one-year anniversary we wanted to go big. Big features, big changes, a HUGE changelog, and A LOT of bugfixes. There's over four months of changes that are in this release, which includes the unreleased Ideology edition (this was meant to be two different editions, whoops). With the game as stable as it is, we are now able to market the game to other markets, mainly the omorashi and AB/DL communities.

For the past few months, we have been working vigorously to have the eraNAS family to be stable and while we're at it, adding some highly requested features as well.  We now have a working religion system where Touhous can change their beliefs with your involvement, a player housing system ported from LiG with several new additions, and a optimization of the panty thieving system to integrate soiling mechanics.

There's also now underwear preferences and a reworked punishment point system where you can now give other touhous punishment points if you see them wet or messy.

# Religion System
Touhous now have a religion that they believe in, influencing many of the actions that they can do. Some may be more outgoing to some aspects, while others will choose not to do an activity due to what they believe in. How they react is determined by their certainty in that religion as well as their faith to it. Certainty determines how much they believe in the religion, while Faith determines how well they follow the religious code. If either hits 0, they'll have a crisis of belief and believe in another religion.

It's also possible to create your own religion by going to an altar or place where you can pray such as the Hakurei and Moriya shrines, as well as the church in Makai. The name and precepts of your region can be changed to suit your needs. After that, you can try converting other characters to your religion.

# Player Housing
We have ported the player housing system from TW-LiG, which allows you to buy a plot of land and upgrade it to support more characters living in it. There are five tiers of houses that can be built on the land, and each of them allow you to do more things in your home. You can invite characters with a fall state into your home, and they'll live in your area, allowing you to give homeless characters a home. Once your home is big enough, you can remodel some of the rooms in a way that drastically changes how they can be used.

# Panty Rework
The previous panty system that was being used only had a safe limit of 100 panties due to the flags outside that range being used for other stuff, and we are way past that limit. We moved to starting at CFLAG 1000, however this conflicted with dialogue and such, a new system had to be made to store the player's panty collection. 

We have now moved to using datatables to store the player's panty collection. This is a faster system that also doesn't have a cap unlike CFLAGs and DIM variables. Since every panty is unique, that means each one one can also store soiling and value data. With this change comes another huge change to praying. Instead of grabbing a high number of low value panties to be sacrificed at an altar, one higher value panty can be used instead. If the panties are soiled, then it increases it's value significantly.

Since soiling values are stored to each panty, that means that you will have to clean them if you don't want to be caught with a dirty clothing bin. Alternatively, you can also just buy more or upgrade your bins to store more dirty panties.
