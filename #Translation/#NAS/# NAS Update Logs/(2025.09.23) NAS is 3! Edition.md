Did you know that eraNAS is now 3 years old? Damn. Here is another release of the game with a bunch of new toys you can play with, mainly a bunch of new stuff. If I didn't get burnt out this would be released back when eraNAS was still only 2 years old. Better late than never I guess.

This is part 1 of Megami Haisetsu, the combat rework of eraNAS. The next edition, Gensokyo Xtreme Fighter, is the the replacement EasyBattle for the new Legend of Gensokyo mode, a Tokyo Xtreme Racer-like mode where you fight rivals and become a stronger fighter as you defeat teams, bosses, and advance through stages.

# Major Changes

## Magic Rework
**PREVIOUS MID DAY SAVES WILL BE FUCKY**

**ALL CHARACTERS WILL HAVE THEIR BODY PARTS RESET**

**NEW CHARACTERS ARE ADDED, YOU MUST RUN UPDATE**

### Reworked the magic system
- Spells from ALL magic schools are now included.
- Spells can now be used in combat.
- Touhous now has resistances and weaknesses to magic
    - Frail: 4x damage
    - Very Weak: 2x damage
    - Weak: 1.5x damage
    - Resist 75% damage
    - High Resist: 50% Damage
    - Extreme Resist: 25% damage
    - Extra Resist: 10% damage
    - Null: 0% damage
    - Drain: 0% damage, damage heals instead
    - Reflect: 0% damage, attack is casted onto the caster.
- Youjutsu schools can be learned by either training with a trainer, using Share-a-skill.com, using youjutsu from an enabler enough times, or by self learning after gaining the conditions needed and solving a puzzle

### A complete weapon rework

**NEARLY ALL WEAPONS HAVE BEEN REMOVED**

There was just too many weapons in the game, ultimately bloating the game's performance. I've decided to go with the nuclear option and remove almost every weapon in the game.
Here are the replacement weapons so that each weapon now has a useful role.

***Most weapon presets have also been removed as well! There are still weapon presets for weapons that can be converted into different types however, such as outsider variants.**

Weapons from Tatara Manufacturing are current not in yet, but will be with added when Kogasa's questline gets added.

Performance goes in ascending order. This means the least powerful weapons are at the top
