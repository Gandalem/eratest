## Stage 1 Part 1 2026.01.20
Welcome to the first part of Legend of Gensokyo, the new mode of eraNAS! 

Legend of Gensokyo is basically my attempt at cumulating most of the work of eraNAS into a playable game with a start and a finish and a near-strict identity. Instead of dating Touhou girls in Gensokyo you're in there instead to beat up rivals to show off that you're the strongest in a perpetual dick measuring contest that everyone's in. There's around 850 rivals in the game, including the ~180 Touhou characters where most of them are teamless wanderers (or bosses). It's planned to be the main mode of eraNAS once the mode is done. If you played Shutokou Battle (Tokyo Xtreme Racer here in the states), you'll get the gist here.

There are teams that roam around certain parts of Gensokyo, beat all the members and you will be challenged by the leader of that particular team. Once you have defeated the boss, you move on to the next one. Once you have defeated all teams in an area you will be presented with a miniboss. They are sometimes tough and other times are just pushovers. Defeating bosses will give you the right to buy their weapon (and even cosplay as them!). Once that area's boss has been defeated, move on to the next area to continue.

Right now however there's only one location, which is the Human Village. The mode is playable up to the first miniboss, #13 The Big Fairy. There are five teams which you need to beat before you get the first miniboss:
- Musket Guy: Musket-only team, mains Akatsuki Banhiken tanegashimas
- Little Gang: Mains Akatsuki Ansatsu revolver muskets
- FAEE: Little fairies with Danmaku knowledge
- Shooting Funs: Pistol musket team
- Real Echo: A team of wolf girls that use the wolf claws as weapons

Another fun thing is Danmaku World! Combatants will post threads on the site giving you info such as that they're ready to challenge you, rage on their inevitable defeat, tips on how to play, give you insightful lore, and to just shitpost if they're bored.
