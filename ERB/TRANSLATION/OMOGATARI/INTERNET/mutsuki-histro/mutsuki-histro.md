#  【YMKA TenguTect one】￥600 (1 pack, released around ~1870)

Flat oshime type diaper made out of terry cloth which keeps the user feeling dry.

Made to be used by Crow Tengu of the Youkai Mountain Tengu, who are incontinent due to their cloacas.

Most popular diaper in Gensokyo until the rollout of disposables. Despite it being released way before the formation of Gensokyo, the TenguTect one is still being manufactured as of Winter 2022.

150x150cm square size fits adults. TenguTect one is easily stackable for heavier continence issues, such as 2x for longer wearing sessions or 4x for nighttime use. It can also be used as a booster for other disposables for a hybrid diapering solution.

To put on a flat cloth diaper, fold it into a triangle flat and put it on, then adhere it via pins. A diaper cover is required or pee will leak.

#  【Gensou-chan FantastaBab】￥2,400 (12 pack, released in 1992)
Gensou-chan's first diaper, a thick ABDL diaper with sizes range from newborn to giants.

Released in 1992 as Gensou-chan's first product, when Shinki's daughter, Alice, started having severe bedwetting leakage issues. Cloth diapers, common in Gensokyo, had to be stacked 6 to 8 fold to have enough protection for her, which made Alice have to crawl. Common disposable options such as Unimu's Tapeman did not provide enough protection causing many leaks.
Shinki designed the FantastaBab primarilly as an ABDL fetish diaper, ready for extended wear and bedwetters.

She had four main goals:
- Have enough padding to be present and keep Alice dry but not be bulky
- Have thick plastic backing to mitigate the smell of poop accidents
- Be extremely comfortable to wear
- Have cute prints.

An absorbency rating of 3000ml and thick plastic backing gave incontinent wearers more time before having to change. Four hook and loop tapes makes adjusting fit during wear and potty time easy.
Front and back wasitbands hug the user tight, tall leak guards keep huge messes, most often caused by Coprodyne, a spell that intentionally caused messy accidents, inside.
The controversial Vivi stay-dry layer kept the wearer feeling dry, while also also making it easy for the wear to use the diaper, most often by accident when the wearer zoned out.

Alongside the release of the FantastaBab was the Omohou line of prints that each FantastaBab in a pack randomly had. These prints included notable residents of Gensokyo, the Lunar Capital, and Makai.

Some example prints:
- Reimu「Toddler of Paradise」: The legendary shrine maiden Hakurei Reimu is depected as an ABDL diaper girl, playing with toys, sitting on a ying yang orb, eating rice with her hands, and flying. White and red diaper
- Sakuya「Blue Yumeko」: This print has Izayoi Sakuya, a maid in the Scarlet Devil Mansion carrying dishes, cooking, playing with a watch, and some fetishized prints of her such as her taking off her diaper and another one where she's pooping herself. White and blue diaper
- Suwako「MoriPoko」: This print immitates an Unimu Tapeman design, replacing its characters with the Suwa clan god Moriya Suwako. The front has a diapered Suwako playing with bugs, while the back has her squatting. White diaper 
- Plain White「Muku」: A plain white diaper

All prints included a yellow to blue wetness indicator line that spans all over the diaper's crotch area.
